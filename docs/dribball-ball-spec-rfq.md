## 1. What we're making
The Dribball is the official ball for a new full-contact sport that blends soccer and basketball. Players **kick it** (soccer), **bounce-dribble it by hand** (basketball), **throw it**, and play **full contact**. The ball must therefore be:

- **Soft and safe** — it strikes players' bodies and hands during full-contact play. No hard core.
- **Bouncy** — it must rebound well enough to be hand-dribbled like a basketball.
- **Durable** — used outdoors and in gyms, kicked repeatedly.
- **Grippable** — catchable and throwable with one hand.
- **Brandable** — a distinctive mustard-yellow with a printed logo.

> ⚠️ **Core design tension we need your help with:** softness (for contact) works against bounce (for dribbling). Please advise on the construction and foam formulation that best balances a **soft, safe feel** with **high rebound**.

## 3. Construction options to quote
Please provide pricing and a recommendation for each option you can produce:

1. **Coated PU foam (solid, self-skinning)** — softest and safest; medium bounce.
2. **High-rebound PU foam (solid)** — same process, foam tuned for more bounce.
3. **Foam-skinned bladder** — inflatable core with a soft foam cover; best bounce, still padded.

For each, note trade-offs in softness, rebound, durability, and cost.

### Internal notes (remove before sending to suppliers)
- **Validate feel before tooling.** Buy off-the-shelf coated-foam balls in 2–3 densities/sizes and play-test against the rules (especially the hand-dribble) to lock diameter, weight, and rebound.
- **Cheap MVP:** buy blank mustard/yellow coated-foam balls and pad-print the logo to get branded Dribballs in hand fast, before committing to a custom mold.
- **Sourcing:** Alibaba / Made-in-China search terms — "custom PU foam ball," "coated foam sports ball," "high rebound foam ball," "custom stress/sports ball manufacturer."
- Typical custom coated-foam unit cost ~$1–4 at volume, plus one-time tooling (~hundreds to low-thousands USD); custom MOQs often 300–1,000.
